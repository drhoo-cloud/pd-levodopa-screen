RUN LOG
Genome screening for levodopa compatibility and gut-brain axis support

Read this file first. It is the order in which the analysis was run and the
date each step was run on. The verbatim step-by-step record of the first two
stages, written in Korean by the laboratory, is kept unedited as
run_log_laboratory_record.txt; this file is the English record of the whole chain.

Every threshold named below was fixed before any genome was retrieved. The
pre-specification record and the two corrections made after it are
Supplementary Methods 2.


1. THRESHOLDS AND REFERENCE SET                              August 2026
   Four reviewed bacterial tyrosine decarboxylases taken from UniProtKB,
   aligned with MAFFT L-INS-i, profile built with hmmbuild.
   Self-score 1342.2 (refs/self_score.txt). A hit is called present above
   half that score. The canonical operon additionally requires tyrS and
   tyrP within 20 kb. Gate 1 counts distinct glycoside hydrolase families
   called by at least two of dbCAN's three methods.
   Files: refs/profile.hmm, refs/reference_proteins.faa,
          refs/reference_alignment.aln, refs/gate2_profile.json

2. FIRST SCREEN, 200 GENOMES PER SPECIES                     August 2026
   22 species, 5,372 genomes. This is the design a laboratory without the
   compute for a census would adopt, and Supplementary Table S4a tests it
   against the census that followed.

3. PRE-GATE ON THE CONTROL GENOMES                           August 2026
   25 Enterococcus genomes screened against CARD and VFDB, every hit placed
   in its genomic context. 822 hits.
   Files: data/pregate_context.tsv, data/pregate_per_genome.tsv
   Rerun: scripts/verify_pregate.py

4. CORRECTIONS                                            September 2026
   Two corrections to the calling rules, both recorded in Methods 2 and in
   run_log_laboratory_record.txt.

5. GATE 2 EXTENDED TO EVERY DEPOSITED GENOME              5 November 2026
   14,399 genomes beyond the 200-per-species cap retrieved and screened with
   pyhmmer against the same profile, E <= 1e-5, same 50% threshold. No
   failures. Census total 19,771 genomes, 11,365 carriers.
   Lactiplantibacillus plantarum went from 0/1,521 to 0/1,665.
   File: data/gate2_calls_per_genome.tsv
   That file holds 19,772 rows: Enterococcus faecalis V583
   (GCF_000007785.1) is the control the self-score is defined against and
   is marked in_census = no. Filtering in_census = yes gives the 19,771.

6. GATE 1 EXTENDED TO EVERY DEPOSITED GENOME              5 November 2026
   19,765 genomes. Six assemblies reached one gate but not the other, which
   is why the two denominators differ by six.
   File: data/gate1_gh_families_per_genome.tsv

7. OPERON CONTEXT FOR EVERY CARRIER                       5 November 2026
   All 11,365 carriers placed in context against tyrS, tyrP and nhaC.
   Files: data/locus_context_census.tsv, data/locus_context_by_species.tsv

8. L. PLANTARUM FOLLOW-UP                                 5 November 2026
   The whole species searched for the pyridoxal-dependent decarboxylase
   family PF00282, and PhiSpy run for prophage context.
   Files: data/lp_decarboxylase_census.tsv, data/lp_prophage_census.tsv,
          data/lp_pf00282_in_prophage.tsv

9. DEREPLICATION RECOMPUTED ON THE FULL CENSUS           12 November 2026
   The first dereplication had been computed on the 1,521 genomes available
   when the data were first deposited, before the 144 added at step 5. All
   1,665 were re-clustered: Mash v2.3, sketch -k 21 -s 10000, single linkage
   at Mash distance <= 0.005 (99.5% ANI). 161 independent lineages. The
   zero-prevalence upper bound is 2.33%, replacing 2.40%.
   Files: data/lp_dereplication_census.tsv, data/lp_cluster_membership.tsv,
          data/accessions_lp_1665.txt
   Rerun: scripts/cluster_single_linkage.py

10. TWO-GATE ASSOCIATION RECOMPUTED ON THE CENSUS       12 November 2026
   The models of Table S4b had been run on the 5,372-genome set. Rerun on
   19,765: logistic without a species term, logistic with species fixed
   effects on the 12,273 genomes of the six mixed species, GEE, variance
   partition, Pagel's lambda and PGLS on both marker trees.
   File: data/twogate_census_stats.tsv
   Rerun: scripts/recompute_twogate_census.py
   Trees: refs/rpoB_species_tree.nwk, refs/16S_species_tree.nwk

11. REPOSITORY ASSEMBLED                                 12 November 2026
   Results gathered from the working folders into this single repository,
   line endings normalised to LF, every file listed with its line count,
   size and MD5 in MANIFEST.tsv.
   verify_all.py recomputes 50 of the numbers printed in the paper from the
   files here and reports any that disagree.
