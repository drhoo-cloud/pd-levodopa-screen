[REASON FOR REVISION 1] Gate 1 - dbCAN single method
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Old: 05_search_all.sh called run_dbcan --tools hmmer
       gate1_cazyme.sh ran hmmsearch against dbCAN.hmm directly, bypassing
       run_dbcan
       gate1_fast.sh extracted only the GH models and ran hmmsearch (narrower)
  Problem: using one of the three prediction methods over-counts domain hits
           L. casei, 33 genomes, median GH families 26 (old) vs 21 (2-of-3)
  New: run_dbcan --tools all, counting only rows of overview.txt with
       #ofTools >= 2
       output search/dbcan/overview_consensus.txt
       gate1_fast.sh deleted, so the old path cannot come back

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[REASON FOR REVISION 2] Gate 2 - assignment by best-scoring decoy
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Old: ODC, HDC and GDC included in the reference set as decoys
       after DIAMOND blastp, absent if the top-scoring family was a decoy,
       otherwise present / ambiguous / absent from a combination of identity,
       coverage and margin
  Problem: whether a gene is present cannot be decided by a ranking contest
           between similar profiles. The margin between two group II PLP
           profiles carries no information about whether the target is there,
           and ODC and HDC do not decarboxylate levodopa
  New: profile HMM built from reviewed bacterial TyrDC sequences alone,
       normalized to its own self-score

[PRE-GATE] histidine decarboxylase screening added
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The old pregate.sh searched CARD and VFDB only.
  Section 2 of the manuscript said the pre-gate also looks at histidine
  decarboxylase, but the code did not. The HDC reference existed only inside
  01c_fix_decoys.py and was never used.

  New: DIAMOND search against refs/pregate_hdc.faa (reviewed bacterial HDC
       from UniProt), thresholds identity >= 40%, coverage >= 70%,
       e-value <= 1e-10.
       These are deliberately looser than the CARD/VFDB thresholds (80%/70%).
       A resistance determinant only means something if it is the same gene,
       whereas HDC is a family-level warning, so a miss costs more than a
       false alarm. The result is not a call; it is a reason to refer the
       strain for culture confirmation.

[ADDED 2026-08-24] Gate 1 counting rule settled - dbCAN raw output reviewed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The raw run_dbcan output for the 5,373 genomes was obtained from a co-author
and re-counted per accession.
  Source: dbcan_results.5373_genomes/, one *.CAZymes.tsv per genome, 5,373 files
  Columns: Gene ID, EC#, dbCAN_hmm, dbCAN_sub, DIAMOND, #ofTools,
           Recommend Results, Substrate

The counting rule, in two steps
  1) use only rows with #ofTools >= 2
       that is, at least two of the three methods called the protein a CAZyme
       accepted 399,459 proteins, discarded 497,239
  2) take the family from the 'Recommend Results' column (pipe-separated)
       not the union of the three methods
       GH0 is dbCAN's unclassified placeholder and is excluded

  NOTE: step 2 is the one that matters. The three methods can call the same
        protein a different family.
        Example: E. faecalis V583 WP_002382786.1
          dbCAN_hmm = GH179, dbCAN_sub = GH109, DIAMOND = -, #ofTools = 2
          Recommend Results = GH109_e7
        Counting the union makes this one protein count as two families. The
        error inflates the per-species median by 1-2 families in the
        Enterococcus and Lacticaseibacillus lineages and barely moves the
        others.

Verification (all 5,373 genomes)
  the per-genome distribution of GH family counts matches exactly in 22 / 22
  species
  overall median 17, range 5-34, mean 17.280
  (CAZyme_GH_cnt.csv, the file the manuscript used: median 17, range 5-34,
   mean 17.280)
  L. casei median 21, matching the value the co-author's review was based on
  rows where Recommend Results was empty and the union had to be used:
  0 / 399,459

Candidate rules examined and rejected (recorded for completeness)
  union of the three columns, GH0 excluded   per-species median matches in
                                             18/22; 1-2 higher in four species
  two or more columns agreeing on a family   overall median 15; too strict
  dbCAN_hmm column alone                     overall median 16
  dbCAN_sub column alone                     overall median 16
  DIAMOND column alone                       overall median 15
  excluding particular families (GH179 etc.) not adopted: this would be
                                             choosing the rule after seeing
                                             the result
